Rebuilding Hiredly's Job Search Experience重塑 Hiredly 的求職體驗

Hiredly

Hero placeholder主視覺待補
Final screens — welcome, homepage, and job list — across iOS and Android.最終畫面——歡迎頁、首頁與職缺列表，涵蓋 iOS 與 Android。

Overview專案概述

Hiredly is a Malaysian job platform. Most of its app users are fresh grads applying for the first time, or close to it.

Hiredly 是一個馬來西亞的求職平台，App 使用者多數是應屆畢業生，或是第一次求職不久的人。

The app worked, but people were spending time in it without much of that turning into applications. So we revamped the whole thing, look and feel included.

App 本身能用，但使用者花了時間在裡面，卻沒有轉化成多少應徵動作。於是我們重新設計了整個 App，連視覺風格也一併翻新。

Role角色
Product Designer (1 of 4) · iOS and Android · Launched產品設計師（4 位其中一位）・iOS 與 Android・已上線
Team團隊
4 UI/UX Designers, 1 Product Manager, 2 Front-end Engineers, 1 Back-end Engineer4 位 UI/UX 設計師、1 位產品經理、2 位前端工程師、1 位後端工程師
Tools工具
Figma, FigJam, Lottiefiles, Lottielab

The Problem問題所在

Signing Up Was Too Easy註冊太簡單了

A short onboarding is a good onboarding. Sounds about right, but our profile was too thin to actually apply with. Users only found out what was missing after hitting apply. That's the worst time for an obstacle, right when they're ready to commit.

簡短的新手導覽，聽起來就該是好的。話是沒錯，但我們的個人檔案太單薄，根本不足以拿去應徵。使用者要等到按下應徵鍵之後，才發現資料不夠完整。這正是設下阻礙最糟的時間點，偏偏就在他們終於準備好要行動的那一刻。

The Homepage Was a Tinder Swipe首頁變成了 Tinder 式滑動

The app opened straight into a swipe feed, with no home or overview, just a decision to make. It was our most distinctive feature, but 81% of surveyed users said they'd rather scroll a list. Rejecting a job felt permanent, so the ones people were only half sure about were just gone.

App 一打開就直接進入滑動式的職缺列表，沒有首頁、沒有總覽，只有一個要馬上做的決定。這是我們最具辨識度的功能，但 81% 的受訪使用者表示，他們寧願用清單瀏覽。拒絕一個職缺感覺是永久的，那些使用者只是還沒拿定主意的職缺，就這樣消失了。

“Some jobs I don't mean to reject, just need some time to consider, and may want to have a second look at after looking at other opportunities.”

「有些職缺我不是真的想拒絕，只是需要時間考慮，看過其他機會後可能還想再回頭看看。」

— 2022 user survey

—— 2022 年使用者調查

Job Details Were One Long Scroll職缺詳情是一長串的捲動頁面

Job and company info were stacked on one page, so comparing them meant scrolling up and down. That mattered, since 67% of users said the company profile and environment were what they cared about most, and it was buried at the bottom.

職缺與公司資訊疊在同一個頁面上，要比對兩者就得不斷上下捲動。這件事很重要，因為 67% 的使用者表示，公司檔案與工作環境是他們最在意的因素，而這些資訊卻被埋在頁面最底部。

Applying Left You Hanging應徵之後就沒下文了

You'd submit, see “pending”, and that was it. No indication that a resume was still needed for the application to actually go anywhere.

送出申請後只會看到「待處理」，然後就沒了。完全沒有任何提示告訴你，履歷還沒補上，申請根本不會有下文。

The Process我們是怎麼走到這裡的

Discover探索

We sent out a user survey through marketing and got 400+ responses back, giving us a clear read on how people actually felt about the app.

我們透過行銷管道發出使用者問卷，收到超過 400 份回覆，讓我們清楚了解使用者對這個 App 的真實感受。

Key Takeaways重點發現

81%

prefer a regular scroll list over the swiping motion when looking for jobs.

的使用者在找工作時，比起滑動配對，更偏好一般的清單捲動瀏覽方式。

67%

selected company profile and environment as the most helpful feature.

的使用者認為公司檔案與工作環境是最有幫助的功能。

Hidden features 隱藏的功能

Users weren't aware of features we already had, like undo application, notifications, or certain filters.

使用者根本不知道我們早就有的功能，例如取消應徵、通知，或是某些篩選條件。

Design Audit設計稽核

Alongside the survey, we audited our own app against competitors, mapping every screen and flow in FigJam to see exactly where we stood.

除了問卷之外，我們也把自己的 App 拿去和競品做比較，在 FigJam 上完整整理每個畫面與流程，清楚看出我們的位置。

Ideate發想

With the research and key insights in hand, we jumped into brainstorming the app design, pulling inspiration from work we found online before putting our own ideas down on paper.

有了研究與重點洞察，我們開始腦力激盪整體設計方向——先參考網路上找到的案例，再落實成自己的想法。

Before anyone drew a screen, we ran a design charrette to agree on one direction — four designers working from four instincts would have made four different apps. That direction was then presented to the wider design and engineering team.

在任何人畫出畫面之前，我們先透過一場設計工作坊定調出單一方向——四位設計師若各憑直覺行事，最後只會做出四個不同的 App。這個方向接著分享給更大範圍的設計與工程團隊。

Design設計

Sketches turned into prototypes, with developers involved from the start — a few interaction ideas actually came from them.

草圖進入原型階段，工程師也從一開始就參與其中——有些互動的想法其實是他們提出來的。

Key Insights重點洞察

Visuals Attract 畫面吸引目光

Users notice visuals first. Employer branding needed to surface earlier than the detail page.

使用者最先注意到的是畫面。雇主品牌需要比職缺詳情頁更早出現。

Familiar Beats Clever 熟悉感勝過巧思

The swipe was our differentiator, and our blocker.

滑動式介面曾是我們的差異化賣點，也是我們的絆腳石。

Bring Them Forward 帶到檯面上

Undo, filters, and notifications were already built — nobody knew.

復原、篩選、通知其實都早就做好了，只是沒人知道。

All of it pointed to one goal:

而這一切，最終都指向同一個目標：

Design Goal設計目標

Support users across the whole journey, not just the moment they hit apply.

支持使用者走過整個應徵旅程，而不只是他們按下應徵的那一刻。

The Outcome我們設計了什麼

Everything below is organised the way users actually move through the app.

以下內容依照使用者實際使用 App 的流程來安排。

Before Applying應徵之前

A Place to Land一個真正的落地頁

A new homepage that works as a lobby. Job matches based on your profile and activity, plus quick access to the pages people used most. It works signed in or not, so there's something useful on screen before the app knows anything about you.

一個真正發揮大廳作用的全新首頁。根據你的個人檔案與行為顯示媒合的職缺，並提供常用頁面的快速入口。不論是否登入都能使用，就算 App 還不認識你，畫面上也早已有派得上用場的內容。

A Profile That Becomes a Resume能變成履歷的個人檔案

Most of our users are writing their first resume, or don't have one ready. Onboarding now asks for more, and explains why, and what you fill in generates a downloadable PDF you can apply with.

大多數使用者都是第一次寫履歷，或者根本還沒有履歷。新手導覽現在會詢問更多資訊，並解釋原因；你填寫的內容會直接生成一份可下載的 PDF，讓你能直接拿去應徵。

While Applying應徵過程中

More Than a Job Title不只是一個職缺名稱

Job summaries and company media sit on the list page itself, so you get a sense of the role and the company without opening anything. Search, filters, and undo are where people can actually find them.

職缺摘要與公司相關素材直接顯示在列表頁上，不用點開就能了解職缺與公司概況。搜尋、篩選與復原功能，也都被放在使用者找得到的地方。

Information in the Right Order依序排好的資訊

Sticky tabs between job details and company details, so you can move between the two instead of scrolling through both.

職缺詳情與公司資訊之間改用 Sticky 分頁切換，讓你能直接在兩者之間跳轉，而不用整頁上下捲動。

After Applying應徵之後

Knowing Where You Stand清楚知道自己的進度

Application status and saved jobs live on one page, so there's always somewhere to check. Changed your mind? Undo is right there.

應徵狀態與已儲存的職缺都集中在同一個頁面，隨時都能查看進度。改變心意了嗎？復原功能就在旁邊。

A New Look and Feel全新的視覺風格

The revamp changed the visuals too, not just the flows.

這次改版不只是流程，連視覺風格也一併翻新。

Welcome Screen歡迎畫面

An animated intro, built in Lottie, that introduces the new look and gets people moving instead of waiting.

一段用 Lottie 製作的動畫式開場，為新的視覺風格定調，也讓使用者一開始就能動起來，而不是乾等。

Motion Throughout貫穿全場的動態細節

Empty states, loading, and the small transitions in between all got the same attention.

空狀態、載入畫面，以及畫面之間的細微轉場，也都用心處理過。

Results成果

Across the full revamp, on iOS and Android.

整個改版上線後，涵蓋 iOS 與 Android 兩個平台的成果：

+40% Job applications per user 每位使用者的應徵數
+19% Average high-intent monthly active users 高意圖月活躍使用者平均數

Next Steps下一步

Application rate got us this far, but it only tells us so much. Profile completion and time-to-first-application are next up to track, so we know where users are still getting stuck rather than just whether they got through.

應徵率讓我們走到了這一步，但它只能說明一部分。接下來要追蹤的是個人檔案完成度與從註冊到第一次應徵的時間，這樣才知道使用者卡在哪裡，而不只是有沒有走完流程。

A few habits from this project are also going with me to the next one: agree on one direction before anyone draws a screen, bring developers in early, and check what's already built before designing something new.

這次專案養成的幾個習慣，也會帶到下一個專案裡：在任何人畫出畫面之前先定調方向、盡早找工程師加入、在設計新東西之前先確認手上已經有什麼。
