Rebuilding Hiredly's Job Search Experience重塑 Hiredly 的求職體驗

Hiredly

Hero placeholder主視覺待補
Final screens — welcome, homepage, and job list — across iOS and Android.最終畫面——歡迎頁、首頁與職缺列表，涵蓋 iOS 與 Android。

Overview專案概述

Hiredly is a Malaysian job platform. Most of its app users are fresh grads applying for the first time, or close to it.

Hiredly 是一個馬來西亞的求職平台，App 使用者多數是應屆畢業生，或是第一次求職不久的人。

The app worked, but people were spending time in it without much of that turning into applications. So we revamped the whole thing, look and feel included.

App 本身能用，但使用者花了時間在裡面，卻沒有轉化成多少應徵動作。於是我們重新設計了整個 App，連視覺風格也一併翻新。

The Problem問題所在

Signing Up Was Too Easy註冊太簡單了

Getting into the app took almost nothing. A few fields, done, you're in.

進到 App 幾乎不用花什麼力氣，填幾個欄位，完成，就進去了。

That sounds like good onboarding until you see what happens next. The profile we collected was too thin to actually apply with, so users would browse, find something they liked, hit apply, and only then run into what was missing. Nothing along the way told them their profile wasn't ready.

聽起來像是不錯的新手導覽，直到你看到接下來發生的事。我們收集到的個人檔案太單薄，根本不足以拿去應徵，於是使用者會先瀏覽、看到喜歡的職缺、按下應徵，然後才發現資料不夠完整。整個過程中，沒有任何提示告訴他們自己的檔案還沒準備好。

For fresh grads especially, that's the worst moment to find out. They're already unsure whether they're good enough for the role, and the app adds another obstacle right at the point of commitment.

對應屆畢業生來說，這是最糟的時間點才發現這件事。他們本來就不確定自己夠不夠格，App 卻在他們終於下定決心的那一刻，又多加了一道阻礙。

The Homepage Was a Tinder Swipe首頁變成了 Tinder 式滑動

Opening the app dropped you straight into a swipe-based job feed. Swipe right to keep, left to reject. No home, no overview, no starting point. The first thing you saw was a decision you had to make.

打開 App，馬上就進入滑動配對式的職缺列表。往右滑保留，往左滑拒絕。沒有首頁、沒有總覽、沒有一個起點——你看到的第一件事，就是一個必須馬上做的決定。

It was our most distinctive feature, and people didn't want it. 81% of surveyed users said they'd rather scroll a list.

這是我們最有辨識度的功能，但使用者不買單。81% 的受訪使用者表示，他們寧願用清單瀏覽的方式。

The problem is that it's a pattern borrowed from somewhere else. Dating apps have endless candidates and low stakes per swipe. Job hunting has neither. Rejecting a job felt permanent, and jobs people were only half sure about were gone.

問題在於，這是從別的地方借來的模式。交友軟體有滑不完的對象，每次滑動的成本都很低；找工作既沒有滑不完的職缺，成本也不低。拒絕一個職缺感覺是永久的，而那些使用者只是還在猶豫的職缺，就這樣消失了。

“Some jobs I don't mean to reject, just need some time to consider, and may want to have a second look at after looking at other opportunities.”

「有些職缺我不是真的想拒絕，只是需要時間考慮，看過其他機會後可能還想再回頭看看。」

— 2022 user survey

—— 2022 年使用者調查

Job Details Were One Long Scroll職缺詳情是一長串的捲動頁面

Job info and company info were stacked on a single page, so comparing them meant scrolling up, then down, then up again.

職缺資訊與公司資訊疊在同一個頁面上，要比對兩者，就得先往下捲，再往上捲，然後再往下捲一次。

That mattered more than it sounds. 67% of users told us company profile and environment were what they cared about most when deciding where to apply, and it was buried at the bottom.

這件事比聽起來更嚴重。67% 的使用者告訴我們，公司檔案與工作環境是他們決定要不要應徵時最在意的因素，而這些資訊卻被埋在頁面最底部。

Applying Left You Hanging應徵之後就沒下文了

You'd submit, see “pending”, and that was it. No indication that a resume was still needed for the application to actually go anywhere.

送出申請後只會看到「待處理」，然後就沒了。完全沒有任何提示告訴你，履歷還沒補上，申請根本不會有下文。

The Process我們是怎麼走到這裡的

Discover探索

We sent out a user survey through marketing and got 400+ responses back, giving us a clear read on how people actually felt about the app.

我們透過行銷管道發出使用者問卷，收到超過 400 份回覆，讓我們清楚了解使用者對這個 App 的真實感受。

Key Takeaways重點發現

81%

prefer a regular scroll list over the swiping motion when looking for jobs.

的使用者在找工作時，比起滑動配對，更偏好一般的清單捲動瀏覽方式。

67%

selected company profile and environment as the most helpful feature.

的使用者認為公司檔案與工作環境是最有幫助的功能。

Hidden features 隱藏的功能

Users weren't aware of features we already had, like undo application, notifications, or certain filters.

使用者根本不知道我們早就有的功能，例如取消應徵、通知，或是某些篩選條件。

Design Audit設計稽核

Alongside the survey, we audited our own app against competitors, mapping every screen and flow in FigJam to see exactly where we stood.

除了問卷之外，我們也把自己的 App 拿去和競品做比較，在 FigJam 上完整整理每個畫面與流程，清楚看出我們的位置。

Key Insights重點洞察

Visuals Attract 畫面吸引目光

Users notice visuals first. Employers need room to stand out through branding and culture.

使用者最先注意到的是畫面，雇主需要能透過品牌與文化脫穎而出的空間。

Simple Works 簡單就好

No need to reinvent the wheel — just make existing features intuitive and easy to use.

不需要重新發明輪子，把既有功能做得直覺好用就夠了。

Navigation 路徑設計

Some useful features already existed — they were just hidden. Bring them forward.

有些好用的功能其實早就存在，只是被藏了起來，該把它們帶到檯面上。

Ideate發想

With the research and key insights in hand, we jumped into brainstorming the app design, pulling inspiration from work we found online before putting our own ideas down on paper.

有了研究與重點洞察，我們開始腦力激盪整體設計方向——先參考網路上找到的案例，再落實成自己的想法。

All of it pointed to one goal:

而這一切，最終都指向同一個目標：

Design Goal設計目標

To ensure users get support and a good experience even before and after making applications.

確保使用者在應徵前後，都能獲得支持與良好的體驗。

Design設計

With that goal set, sketches turned into prototypes, developers involved from the start — a few of the interaction ideas were actually theirs.

確立目標後，草圖進入原型階段，工程師也從一開始就參與其中——有些互動的想法其實是他們提出來的。

The Outcome我們設計了什麼

New Design Direction歡迎畫面

This is the direction we landed on in the charrette, reviewed with the wider design and engineering team and signed off before a single production screen was built.

這是我們在工作坊中定調的視覺方向，經過更大範圍的設計與工程團隊審視並拍板後，才開始正式製作任何畫面。

Everything below follows that direction, starting with the first thing users see.

以下所有畫面都依循著這個方向而生——從使用者看到的第一個畫面開始。

Welcome Screen歡迎畫面

An animated intro that sets the new look and feel.

一段動畫式的開場，為新的視覺風格定調。

Onboarding新手導覽

Longer than before, and clearer about why. You finish with a profile you can actually apply with.

比之前更長，也把原因說得更清楚。填完之後，你手上就是一份真的能拿去應徵的個人檔案。

Homepage首頁

An actual landing page. Job matches and quick access to what matters, built for both logged-in users and visitors, so it works before it knows anything about you.

一個真正的到達頁。展示媒合的職缺並提供常用功能的快速入口；不論是已登入的使用者還是訪客都能使用，就算 App 還不認識你，也一樣好用。

List Instead of Swipe用清單取代滑動

Job summaries and company media on the list itself, filters and undo where people can find them. Nothing disappears permanently.

職缺摘要與公司相關素材直接放在列表上，篩選與復原功能也放在使用者找得到的地方，沒有任何東西會真的消失。

Readable Detail Pages好讀的詳情頁

Sticky tabs between job and company info, and a profile that generates a downloadable resume.

職缺與公司資訊之間用了 Sticky 分頁切換，個人檔案也能直接產生可下載的履歷。

Results成果

Across the full revamp, on iOS and Android.

整個改版上線後，涵蓋 iOS 與 Android 兩個平台的成果：

+40% Job applications per user 每位使用者的應徵數
+19% Average high-intent monthly active users 高意圖月活躍使用者平均數

Next Steps下一步

Application rate got us this far, but it only tells us so much. Profile completion and time-to-first-application are next up to track, so we know where users are still getting stuck rather than just whether they got through.

應徵率讓我們走到了這一步，但它只能說明一部分。接下來要追蹤的是個人檔案完成度與從註冊到第一次應徵的時間，這樣才知道使用者卡在哪裡，而不只是有沒有走完流程。

A few habits from this project are also going with me to the next one: agree on one direction before anyone draws a screen, bring developers in early, and check what's already built before designing something new.

這次專案養成的幾個習慣，也會帶到下一個專案裡：在任何人畫出畫面之前先定調方向、盡早找工程師加入、在設計新東西之前先確認手上已經有什麼。
